TrustMed RAG: Project Plan
1. What you’re building (in one line)

An AI assistant that answers breast cancer research questions using published papers. It cites its sources, avoids retracted papers, shows how strong the evidence is, and flags any sentence it can’t back up.

2. How RAG works (the core idea)
Store: split research papers into small chunks, turn each chunk into numbers (an embedding), and save them in a vector database.
Retrieve: when a user asks a question, find the chunks most similar to it.
Generate: give those chunks plus the question to an LLM, which writes an answer using only that information, with citations.

Your project adds three “trust” layers on top: a retraction check, evidence strength, and claim verification.

3. Project requirements

Functional (what it must do)

Answer breast cancer research questions in plain English
Cite the paper (title, year, link) behind each claim
Exclude or flag retracted papers
Show an evidence strength badge (strong / moderate / weak)
Mark each sentence as supported ✅ or unsupported ⚠️
Say “not enough evidence” instead of guessing
Refuse personal medical advice and show a disclaimer

Non-functional (how well it must do it)

Answers in under about 10 seconds
Free to build and run
A live public demo anyone can try
Measurable quality, with a results table comparing it against basic RAG

Out of scope (keeping it finishable)

Other cancer types (can be added later)
Patient-facing diagnosis or treatment advice
User accounts or logins

6. Architecture
User question
   ↓
Safety check (refuse personal medical advice)
   ↓
Retrieve chunks from ChromaDB
   ↓
Remove retracted papers  →  Rank by evidence strength
   ↓
LLM writes an answer with citations
   ↓
Claim verifier checks each sentence (✅ / ⚠️)
   ↓
Answer + citations + evidence badge + disclaimer

8. How you’ll measure success
Metric	Meaning
Retrieval recall	Did it find the right papers?
Faithfulness (RAGAS)	Does the answer stick to the retrieved text?
Unsupported-claim rate	Percentage of sentences not backed by a source
Retracted-citation rate	Should be 0% for TrustMed

The headline result for your CV and LinkedIn will look like: “Reduced unsupported claims from X% to Y% compared to standard RAG.”

9. Risks and how to handle them
Risk	Plan
Too much to learn at once	Finish Phase 2 before touching any trust features
Groq free limits run out	Switch to another free API or a small local model
Few retracted breast cancer papers in your sample	Add some deliberately for testing
Medical safety	Researcher-focused, with a clear disclaimer and refusal of personal advice
